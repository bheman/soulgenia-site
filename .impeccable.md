## Design Context

### Users
Soul Genia should speak to a broad first audience by Bruno's direction:
busy people who need help organizing both personal and professional life.
This includes entrepreneurs, solo professionals, parents, creators, executives,
and anyone whose routine is scattered across WhatsApp, email, calendar, groups,
audio messages, bills, reminders, tasks, and personal commitments.

The user should not feel they are buying a CRM, chatbot, sales automation tool,
dashboard, or another productivity app. They are hiring a personal secretary
that lives where their life already happens.

### Use Cases
The core promise is: "uma secretaria pessoal dentro do seu WhatsApp."

Soul Genia can help with:

- Managing WhatsApp conversations and groups.
- Scheduling messages to be sent later.
- Asking for approval before sending messages.
- Sending reminders for bills, medicine, commitments, tasks, and routines.
- Producing morning briefings.
- Organizing personal and professional calendar context.
- Reading and summarizing email/calendar context when connected.
- Summarizing long conversations and long audio messages.
- Researching topics and bringing practical answers.
- Analyzing and creating images.
- Organizing financial reminders and records when the user teaches her the flow,
  without promising automatic payments.

The first onboarding is assisted. The user starts by talking to Soul Genia,
teaching their routine, and letting the assistant learn over time.

### Brand Personality
Soul Genia should feel human, discreet, capable, protective, and quietly
magical. It should feel like a trusted secretary with memory and initiative,
not a tech product trying to impress with AI jargon.

The public brand is Soul Genia. Auria may remain as an internal character/name
for now, but the landing should lead with Soul Genia.

### Aesthetic Direction
The interface should sell relief and trust: "I do not need another app; I can
delegate to someone who already understands my WhatsApp life."

Avoid SaaS/CRM tropes, dashboards, sales-pipeline visuals, chatbot builder
language, and generic "AI agent for sales" positioning. The page should feel
personal, conversational, and premium-service oriented.

Use visual storytelling around everyday life: morning briefing, unread groups,
long audio, calendar conflict, bill reminder, medication reminder, scheduled
message, and an approval step before any external send.

### Design Principles
- Lead with relief: less mental load, fewer forgotten tasks, fewer loose ends.
- Make WhatsApp the center of the experience, not a dashboard.
- Say clearly that there is no new app to configure.
- Emphasize approval and trust before autonomy.
- Present Soul Genia as a professional that learns with the user over time.
- Keep the first CTA conversational: "falar com a Soul Genia" or "comecar pelo WhatsApp".
- Do not promise automatic payments or uncontrolled sending.
- Preserve the future ad angle: scheduled message sending is a major
  differentiator Bruno wants to explore later.
